Refund Policy
Last updated: May 23, 2024

Introduction

BarnacleAI PTE LTD is committed to your satisfaction. If you have purchased digital/hard goods/subscriptions from BarnacleAI PTE LTD and are unhappy with the product received, you may be eligible for a refund/partial refund if requested within 30 days of the original purchase date.

Refunds of Hard Goods:

To be eligible for return and refund, the following steps must be taken:

1. Refund must be requested in writing by contacting support@barnacleai.com.
2. Request for a refund must be made within 30 days of the original purchase date.
3. Hard goods must be returned to BarnacleAI PTE LTD immediately, according to the instructions you will receive once contacting support@barnacleai.com as directed in step 1.

Refunds of Digital/Subscription Based Goods:

To be eligible for a refund on any digital/subscription-based goods, the following steps must be taken:

1. Refund must be requested in writing by contacting support@barnacleai.com.
2. Request for a refund must be made within 30 days of the original purchase date.

BarnacleAI PTE LTD is committed to its consumers, and while we stand by our policy as written above, we also want to understand how we can resolve your dissatisfaction and better understand how we can serve you. Please contact BarnacleAI PTE LTD at support@barnacleai.com for any questions related to our policy, or simply to let us know how we can help.
